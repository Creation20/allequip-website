Keeping vehicles and machines working

AllEquip Spares & Supplies is an Accra-based supplier of spare parts, lubricants and hydraulic components for vehicles, trucks and machinery.

Who we are

What AllEquip is all about

Every day, businesses across Ghana depend on vehicles and equipment to deliver goods, build, farm and serve customers.

When something breaks, the cost isn't just the part: it's every hour the vehicle or machine stands idle. AllEquip was set up to be the supplier those businesses can rely on.

We keep the parts that are asked for most in stock, source the ones that aren't, and give every customer a clear, itemised quotation so they can decide quickly. Whether it's one brake pad set for a workshop or a recurring order for a fleet, every request gets the same care.

How we work
