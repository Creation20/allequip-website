FAQs

Questions we're often asked

Can't find your answer? Contact our sales team

How do I get a price for a part?

Use the quote form, or email us with the part number and quantity. If you don't have a part number, send the make, model and year of the vehicle or machine, or a photo of the old part, and we'll identify it.

Do you only sell vehicle parts?

No. Alongside car and truck parts we supply hydraulic components, filtration, lubricants and service parts for plant and machinery.

Can you find parts you don't have in stock?

Yes. We source parts on request. Send us the details and we'll come back with availability, price and an expected date.

Are your parts genuine?

We supply genuine and quality OEM-equivalent parts from trusted manufacturers, and we'll always tell you which you're getting.

How can I pay?

Cash, Mobile Money, bank transfer and cheque. Account customers can be set up with agreed credit terms.

Do you issue VAT invoices?

Yes. We're VAT-registered, and every sale comes with a proper invoice showing VAT, NHIL and GETFund.

Do you deliver?

You can collect from us in Accra, or we can arrange delivery to your workshop, depot or site. Ask when you request your quote.
